Sunstop Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Summary

Sunstop has no accounts, uses no ads or analytics, and the developer never receives or stores any personal information.

Camera

Used only to measure the brightness of the scene on screen and calculate exposure. Photos and video are never saved or sent off the device.

Location

  • Sunstop uses your approximate location to calculate exposure from the sky where you are (satellite irradiance). Without location access, it calculates for the main city of your device's time zone.
  • To get forecast and satellite values, coordinates rounded to about 5 km are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). No location is sent when downloading GK2A satellite data (Korea only).
  • The neighborhood name shown in the app is looked up with Apple's map services.
  • Your location never reaches the developer and isn't recorded on the device after the calculation (only the last result and place name are briefly kept on the device).

Stored only on your device

Settings such as film speed and base aperture, and recent weather calculations. They're deleted when you delete the app.

Third-party services

  • GitHub (github.io, githubusercontent.com): hosts the GK2A satellite data file (downloaded only in Korea). Standard access logs (such as IP address) may be kept on GitHub's servers.
  • Open-Meteo: forecast and satellite values, with approximate coordinates as described above.
  • Apple: location and place-name lookup.

Children

Sunstop doesn't collect personal information from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
